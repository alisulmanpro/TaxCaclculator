How it works

How tax is worked out across several years

Pakistan taxes each fiscal year on its own, from 1 July to 30 June. Your salary history is cut into those years first.

1

Each period is split at 30 June

A period that runs past the year end is cut in two, so each part lands in the right fiscal year.

2

Only the days you worked count

If you started or left part-way through a month, that month is counted by its days.

3

Each year is added up, then taxed

Salary from every period in one fiscal year is added together and taxed once, with that year's rates.
